Android pins JamesQiu2005/SpektraLab at 445e314. The retained halation_psf.hpp and params.hpp bytes match the Windows pin.

Code: GPL-3.0-or-later. Original copyright/author notices are retained in research/spektralab, including all README acknowledgements and original licences. Contributions and modifications for Lumatte: GameChicks, 2026.

Adapted: four signed Gaussian PSFs, 120um base, strengths, highlight headroom gates, FIR/Young-van-Vliet dispatch and recurrence, 135 stock-specific edge lettering/glyphs/DX geometry, seven-segment date geometry. Derived edge-faces.json carries the printer glyphs and labels/DX excerpt from overscan.cpp / filmEdge.ts. DX stock identifiers are sourced upstream from The Big Film Database (CC BY-SA 4.0) and NegativeConverter; preserve that attribution. Some upstream stock layouts are borrowed or unverified; they are not measured profiles for every stock.

Differences: Lumatte retains its Fotufilm colour/development/receiver models and its existing grain/black-mist lens. CPU double replaces GPU double-float recurrence. The CineStill spatial grid is full render resolution. 400D shares the 800T mechanism with its own colour model; the pinned repositories do not supply a separate 400D reference profile. Export borders and date stamps are composed AFTER rendering; ink/base colours are presentation colours, not a chemical border simulation. No Vulkan/Metal runtime, original application binaries or test suite is bundled. No output or device parity has been established.

Fonts are original TeX Gyre Heros and DejaVu Sans Mono bytes, under the adjacent GUST/DejaVu licences.
